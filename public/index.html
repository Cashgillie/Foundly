How Foundly Works

Three simple steps to reunite lost items with their owners.

Step 2

Lost an Item?

Here's exactly what
you need to do

Follow this journey to report your missing item and stay informed at every step — via email.

Step 1

Log In

Sign in to your Foundly account (or register in under a minute) so the report is tied to you.

Step 2

Fill the Form

Describe your item — category, location, and what happened. Leave one detail out on purpose.

Step 3

Submit Report

Add your contact email and submit. Your report goes live immediately (contact hidden from the public).

Step 4

Get Confirmation

You'll get an email with your report details and a private edit link right away.

Step 5

Stay Notified

If the office matches your item to something found, they'll email you to come identify it.

Email Notifications

We'll send updates to the email you report with — your confirmation + edit link, an invite to come identify a match, and the final resolved notice.

Private by Default

Your contact email is never shown to other users on this site — only the Foundly office can see it, so you won't get random messages from strangers.

Found an Item?

Do the right thing —here's how to help

If you've found an item on campus, follow these steps to get it back to its rightful owner through the school's Lost & Found Department.

1
Step 1

Locate the Office

Bring the item to the GCTU Lost & Found Department — Main Admin Block, Ground Floor.

2
Step 2

Hand It In

Give the item to the administrator on duty. They'll tag it and store it securely until the owner is found.

3
Step 3

Leave Your Details

Optionally leave your contact info with the office — we'll only use it to thank you once the item is reunited.

4
Step 4

Await Feedback

The office lists the item on Foundly. Once the rightful owner claims it, your good deed is on record.

Safety Tips

Leave out one identifying detail

When you report a lost item, don't describe every single detail. Keep one identifying feature back — you'll use it at the office to prove the item is yours.

All handovers happen at the Foundly office

We never arrange private meetups. Come to the department in person, describe your item fully, and our team will confirm and hand it over.

Never share sensitive info publicly

Don't post your home address, ID numbers, or financial info in a report's description.

Your contact info stays private

Only the Foundly team can see the email you report with. It's never displayed to other users.

Report suspicious listings

If a report looks fake, scammy, or inappropriate, use the "Report suspicious" option on that item so our team can review it.

Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here's the short version.

What we collect

When you register, we store your email, name, and (optionally) phone number. When you report a lost item, we store the item details, location, and your contact email.

Who can see your contact info

Your contact email is never shown to other users. Only the Foundly office (admin) can see it, so they can reach you if your item is found or if there's a question about your report.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to operate the lost & found service — matching reports, contacting you about your items, and department record-keeping. We don't sell or share it for marketing.

Your rights

You can update your profile at any time, delete a report you made, or ask us to remove your account entirely by contacting the office.
