How Foundly Works

Please read this before you get started, it explains exactly how lost & found reports move through Foundly.

1

You report what you lost, that's the only thing users report

Only the person who lost an item creates a report on Foundly. There's no public "I found this" form — that keeps the Found list trustworthy and prevents fake or joke listings.

2

The Foundly team uploads found items

When someone hands in an item at the Lost & Found office, our team lists it on the Found page themselves. If you found something, please bring it to the office rather than posting about it here.

3

Contact details are private, only the office can see them

Both Lost and Found listings are visible to everyone, but the email address behind each report is hidden from other users. Only the Foundly office can see it, so no one can be contacted directly through the site.

4

Come to the office to identify your item

Spotted a match? Don't just message someone, visit the Foundly office in person and describe the item in full detail, including anything you deliberately left out of your original report.

5

The Foundly team confirms the handover

Our team checks your description against the item before handing anything over. This protects real owners from false ownership claims.

6

The report is marked Resolved, everyone gets notified

Once the handover is confirmed, the report is marked resolved. If we have contact info for both the person who lost it and whoever found it, we email both to let them know it's settled.

See something that looks fake, inappropriate, or suspicious? Open the item and use Report Suspicious, our team reviews every flagged listing.
